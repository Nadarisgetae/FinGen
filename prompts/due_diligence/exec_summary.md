Write the EXECUTIVE SUMMARY section of a Due Diligence report.

Structure:
1. Scope: documents reviewed (by type and fiscal year), company identity.
2. Headline financials: latest revenue, PAT, net debt — cite with period and unit.
3. Red flag count: errors and warnings raised, in one sentence.
4. Overall DD posture: "proceed / proceed with conditions / further work required" — framed as a recommendation, not a verdict.

Rules:
- Cite every figure with period and unit.
- Max 300 words.
