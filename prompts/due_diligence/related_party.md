Write the RELATED-PARTY TRANSACTIONS section of a Due Diligence report.

Structure:
1. What the source documents disclose about related-party transactions, quoted or closely paraphrased with the source page.
2. Patterns: volume, nature, and counterparties of related-party dealings — only as disclosed.
3. Assessment: whether disclosure appears complete relative to what the documents state. Do not infer undisclosed dealings.

Rules:
- Draw ONLY on narrative snippets from the source documents (context). Cite page numbers where available.
- If no related-party disclosures exist in the provided documents, state that plainly.
- Max 350 words.
