Write the CONTINGENT LIABILITIES & LITIGATION section of a Due Diligence report.

Structure:
1. Disclosed contingencies: material pending litigation, guarantees, contingent liabilities — as disclosed, with amounts and status where stated.
2. Quantification: aggregate disclosed amounts vs net worth, if both are available. Cite figures with period.
3. Gaps: areas where disclosure is absent or vague.

Rules:
- Draw ONLY on narrative snippets from the source documents. Cite page numbers where available.
- Never estimate undisclosed liabilities. If nothing is disclosed, state that.
- Max 350 words.
