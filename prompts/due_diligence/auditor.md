Write the AUDITOR NOTES & QUALIFICATIONS section of a Due Diligence report.

Structure:
1. Auditor opinion: as stated in the source documents (e.g., unmodified/modified). Quote the opinion language where available.
2. Key audit matters / emphasis of matter: as disclosed, with page citations.
3. Qualifications: any modified opinions, qualifications, or going-concern references — verbatim or closely paraphrased.

Rules:
- Draw ONLY on narrative snippets from the source documents. Cite page numbers.
- If the auditor's report is not among the provided documents, state that. Do not infer the opinion.
- Max 300 words.
