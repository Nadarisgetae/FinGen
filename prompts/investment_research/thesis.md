Write the INVESTMENT THESIS section of an Investment Research report.

Structure:
1. The core thesis in 2-3 sentences: what must happen for this investment to work.
2. Evidence: growth rates, margins, returns that support the thesis. Cite each with period.
3. The key assumption the thesis depends on — name it explicitly.

Rules:
- Every number cited with period and unit.
- The thesis is a judgment: frame it as "the thesis holds if..." not as fact.
- Max 400 words.
