Write the RISK / REWARD section of an Investment Research report.

Structure:
1. Downside: the financial risk flags (with metric values, thresholds, severity) and what they imply.
2. Market risk: annualized volatility, beta, max drawdown — only if price history was provided.
3. Reward: the valuation ranges from the valuation section. (estimate)
4. Risk/reward framing: state the asymmetry in terms of the computed ranges, not adjectives.

Rules:
- Cite every metric with its value and basis.
- Max 350 words.
