Write the RISK MITIGANTS section of a Risk Analysis report.

Structure:
1. Balance-sheet mitigants: cash balance, equity cushion, current ratio — cite values with period.
2. Earnings mitigants: EBITDA level and coverage — cite values with period.
3. Honest limits: if no mitigant facts were provided, say so explicitly.

Rules:
- Use ONLY the provided facts. Do not invent mitigants.
- Max 250 words.
