Write the OPERATIONAL & CONCENTRATION RISK section of a Risk Analysis report.

Structure:
1. Revenue concentration: HHI (and DOJ-scale classification), top-1/top-3/top-5 shares.
2. Interpretation: is the company dependent on a single segment, product, or geography? Cite the shares.
3. Operational flags: only those supported by provided facts (e.g., single-customer dependence stated in source documents).

Rules:
- Use computed concentration_risk metrics directly.
- Cite every share as a percentage.
- Max 350 words.
