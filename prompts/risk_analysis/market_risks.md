Write the MARKET RISK section of a Risk Analysis report.

Structure:
1. Volatility: annualized volatility, and what it implies for position sizing.
2. Beta: sensitivity to the benchmark (if price history and benchmark were provided).
3. Drawdown: max drawdown and its duration. VaR (95%, daily).
4. Sharpe ratio: risk-adjusted return vs the risk-free rate assumption.

Rules:
- Use computed market_risk_metrics directly.
- If price history was not provided, state that market risk metrics require price data and omit the section body. Do not invent values.
- Max 300 words.
