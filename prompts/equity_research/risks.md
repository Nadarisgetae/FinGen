Write the KEY RISKS section of an Equity Research report.

Structure:
1. Financial risks: list the flags raised by the risk engine (liquidity, solvency, coverage, profitability) with their severity and the underlying metric values.
2. What would break the thesis: the 2-3 most material flags, stated in terms of the numbers that trigger them.
3. Mitigants: only those supported by facts (e.g., cash balance, equity cushion).

Rules:
- Cite each flag's metric value, threshold, and period.
- Do not soften or inflate severity — report exactly what the flags say.
- Max 350 words.
