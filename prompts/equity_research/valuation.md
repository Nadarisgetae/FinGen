Write the VALUATION section of an Equity Research report.

Structure:
1. DCF: state the WACC, terminal growth assumption, enterprise value, equity value, and implied value. All are model outputs — label "(estimate)".
2. Relative valuation: peer median EV/EBITDA, P/E, P/B and the implied equity values from each multiple.
3. Football field: summarize the valuation ranges (DCF range, multiples range, market range) in one compact table.
4. Sensitivity: how equity value moves with WACC/terminal-growth changes.

Rules:
- Every valuation figure labelled "(estimate)" and tied to its stated assumptions.
- Use computed dcf_fcff, multiples_valuation, football_field metrics directly. Do not recompute.
- Max 500 words.
