Write the FINANCIAL PERFORMANCE section of an Equity Research report.

Structure:
1. Growth: revenue/EBITDA/PAT trajectory with YoY rates and CAGR.
2. Margins: gross/EBITDA/PAT margins per period; expanding or compressing?
3. Common size: key cost lines as % of revenue. Where does the money go?
4. Returns: ROE trend.

Rules:
- Cite every figure with period. Margins in %, absolute figures in ₹ cr.
- Use computed yoy_growth, cagr, profitability_ratios, common_size_statements metrics.
- Max 500 words.
