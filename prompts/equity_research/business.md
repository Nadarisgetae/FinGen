Write the BUSINESS OVERVIEW section of an Equity Research report.

Structure:
1. Business model: what the company sells, to whom, in which segments/geographies.
2. Revenue concentration: top segment shares, HHI-based concentration level. Is the company diversified or concentrated?
3. Strategic position: based ONLY on provided facts. Do not claim market leadership unless a market-share fact supports it.

Rules:
- Cite segment revenue figures with period and unit.
- Use the computed concentration_risk metrics (hhi, top1_share, concentration_level).
- Max 350 words.
