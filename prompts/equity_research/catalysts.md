Write the CATALYSTS & OUTLOOK section of an Equity Research report.

Structure:
1. Near-term catalysts: grounded in guidance facts (revenue guidance, growth guidance, capex guidance) if provided. If no guidance facts exist, say so.
2. Projected trajectory: 5-year projected revenue/EBITDA/PAT from the forecast engine, labelled "(estimate)" and tied to the stated growth/margin assumptions.
3. What to watch: the key assumption variables the projection depends on.

Rules:
- Projections are estimates — always labelled "(estimate)" with the assumption source.
- Never state projections as facts.
- Max 300 words.
