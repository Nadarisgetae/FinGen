Write the INVESTMENT SUMMARY section of an Equity Research report.

Structure:
1. One-paragraph thesis: what the company is, its financial character (growth rate, margin level, leverage), and the central investment question.
2. Snapshot table (markdown): latest revenue, EBITDA, PAT, net debt, ROE — each with period.
3. Stance: state that the rating/target price require analyst review of the assumptions provided. Do NOT assert a rating as fact.

Rules:
- Every number cited with period and unit.
- Mark any assumption-derived figure as "(estimate)".
- Max 300 words.
