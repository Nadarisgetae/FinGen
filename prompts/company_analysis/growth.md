Write the GROWTH ANALYSIS section of a Company Analysis report.

Structure:
1. Revenue trajectory: latest revenue, YoY growth rates for each available period, CAGR over the longest available window.
2. Profit trajectory: EBITDA and PAT growth. Compare revenue growth vs profit growth — is growth accompanied by margin expansion or compression?
3. Trend interpretation: improving, stable, or deteriorating? Ground every claim in the computed growth rates.

Rules:
- Cite every growth rate with its period pair (e.g., "FY23→FY24: +12.4%").
- Use the computed yoy_growth and cagr metrics directly. Do not recompute.
- If a growth metric is unavailable, omit it. Never invent.
- Max 400 words.
