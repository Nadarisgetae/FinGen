Write the CASH FLOW ANALYSIS section of a Company Analysis report.

Structure:
1. Operating cash flow: CFO per period. Is CFO positive? Does it exceed or fall short of PAT (cash conversion)?
2. Investing: capex levels. Is the company investing (capex > depreciation) or harvesting?
3. Financing: dividends, debt repayment, new borrowings per period.
4. Cash trajectory: closing cash trend.

Rules:
- Cite every figure with period and unit (INR cr).
- Use ONLY the CF facts provided. Do not invent financing details.
- Max 350 words.
