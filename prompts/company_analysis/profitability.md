Write the PROFITABILITY section of a Company Analysis report.

Structure:
1. Margin profile: gross margin, EBITDA margin, PAT margin for each period. Trend direction.
2. Returns: ROE, ROCE, ROIC. Are returns above or below typical cost of capital (12-15%)? State the actual figures.
3. DuPont decomposition: ROE = net margin × asset turnover × financial leverage. Identify which driver dominates.

Rules:
- Cite every ratio with period. Ratios are unitless — express as % (margins, returns) or x (turnover, leverage).
- Use the computed profitability_ratios and dupont_analysis metrics directly.
- Max 400 words.
