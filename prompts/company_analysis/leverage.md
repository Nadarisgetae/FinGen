Write the LEVERAGE & LIQUIDITY section of a Company Analysis report.

Structure:
1. Capital structure: total debt, equity, debt/equity ratio per period. Is leverage rising or falling?
2. Net debt position: total debt minus cash. Net Debt/EBITDA in turns (x).
3. Liquidity: current ratio. Comment only on what the current ratio shows — do not speculate about undrawn credit lines unless provided.

Rules:
- Cite every figure with period and unit (INR cr) or ratio (x).
- Use the computed leverage_ratios metrics directly.
- Max 350 words.
