Write the BUSINESS OVERVIEW section of a Company Analysis report.

Structure:
1. What the company does (core business, segments, geographies) — use ONLY segment facts provided.
2. Revenue mix: which segments/geographies contribute what share of revenue. Cite each segment's revenue figure and period.
3. Scale: latest revenue, total assets, equity. Cite exact figures with period and unit.

Rules:
- Every number must cite period (e.g., "FY24") and unit (e.g., "₹ cr").
- If a segment breakdown is unavailable, say "segment data was not provided" and move on. Never invent.
- Objective, third-person, past tense. No promotional language.
- Max 300 words.
