Write the OPERATING EFFICIENCY section of a Company Analysis report.

Structure:
1. Asset productivity: asset turnover (revenue/total assets). Trend.
2. Working capital cycle: inventory days, receivable days, payable days, net WC cycle days. Is the cycle lengthening or shortening?
3. Interpretation: what the WC cycle implies for cash generation. Keep it factual.

Rules:
- Cite every metric with period. Days are in days; turnover in turns (x).
- Use the computed efficiency_ratios metrics directly.
- If inventory/receivables/payables data is missing, state that the WC cycle cannot be computed. Do not invent.
- Max 350 words.
