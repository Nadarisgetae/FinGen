Write the CREDIT OVERVIEW section of a Credit Analysis report.

Structure:
1. Internal rating: grade (AAA-C) and composite score, with the four pillar scores (leverage, coverage, liquidity, profitability).
2. One-paragraph credit character: strong/weak points grounded in the pillar scores.
3. Key credit metrics snapshot table (markdown): Debt/Equity, Net Debt/EBITDA, ICR, current ratio — latest period.

Rules:
- The rating is a model output — present it as "internal model rating", not an agency rating.
- Cite every metric with period.
- Max 300 words.
