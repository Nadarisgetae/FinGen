Write the LIQUIDITY POSITION section of a Credit Analysis report.

Structure:
1. Current ratio per period: short-term asset cover for short-term liabilities.
2. Cash position: cash and equivalents in ₹ cr, and cash as % of total debt.
3. Working capital: current assets minus current liabilities per period.

Rules:
- Cite every figure with period and unit.
- Use ONLY the BS facts provided. Do not infer undrawn facilities.
- Max 300 words.
