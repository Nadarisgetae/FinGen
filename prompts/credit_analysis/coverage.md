Write the COVERAGE & DEBT SERVICE section of a Credit Analysis report.

Structure:
1. Interest coverage (EBITDA/finance costs) per period: cushion above 1.0x.
2. DSCR ((EBITDA - capex)/(interest + principal)) per period: can the company service debt from operations after capex?
3. Trend: improving or deteriorating coverage?

Rules:
- Use computed icr and dscr metrics directly.
- Cite every ratio with period, in x.
- If principal repayment data is missing, state DSCR could not be computed. Do not invent.
- Max 350 words.
