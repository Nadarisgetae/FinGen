Write the KEY ASSUMPTIONS section of a Financial Forecast report.

Structure:
1. Assumption table (markdown): driver, value, unit, period, rationale, source. Pull from the assumptions provided.
2. Historical base: the latest actual revenue/EBITDA the projections build on, with period.
3. Assumption quality: which assumptions are sourced from documents vs user-entered vs model defaults.

Rules:
- Assumptions are inputs, not outputs — present them exactly as given, with their rationale and source.
- Label user-entered assumptions "(assumption)".
- Max 350 words.
