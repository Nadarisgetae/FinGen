Write the ATTRIBUTION section of a Portfolio report.

Structure:
1. Attribution table (markdown): sector, allocation effect, selection effect, interaction effect, total effect.
2. Totals: allocation, selection, interaction, and total active return.
3. Drivers: which sectors contributed most positively and most negatively.

Rules:
- Use computed portfolio_attribution metrics directly.
- Effects in percentage points. Cite the measurement period.
- Max 350 words.
