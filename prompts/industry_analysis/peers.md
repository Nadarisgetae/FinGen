Write the PEER BENCHMARKING section of an Industry Analysis report.

Structure:
1. Benchmark table (markdown): the target company and each peer — revenue, EBITDA margin, PAT margin, ROE, Debt/Equity — latest common period.
2. Percentile position: where the target sits on each metric vs the peer set.
3. Relative position: where the company leads and lags, grounded in the percentile data.

Rules:
- Use computed peer_benchmark metrics directly.
- Cite every metric with the period used.
- If fewer than 2 peers were provided, state that benchmarking requires a peer set. Do not invent peer data.
- Max 450 words.
