Write the INDUSTRY STRUCTURE section of an Industry Analysis report.

Structure:
1. Concentration: HHI, CR4, CR8, and the classification (fragmented / moderately concentrated / highly concentrated).
2. Leader: the largest player's share, if peer shares were provided.
3. Competitive character: what the concentration metrics imply about pricing power and competition.

Rules:
- Use computed industry_concentration metrics directly.
- If no peer share data was provided, state that structure analysis requires competitor share inputs. Do not invent.
- Max 300 words.
